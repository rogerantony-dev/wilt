  • Continuous in the design, stepped in the build. The pill and the widget both draw from PNGs (the widget must; RemoteViews cannot draw), so the app would ship 6 frames instead of 5, one per band above. The strip is exactly those frames.
  • Colour follows the cat. The pill's time stays bone until the mould starts, goes amber as the skull shows, and red once over. The widget already does amber then red; it keeps that.
  • Motion is optional. Flies and stink lines are shown here to sell it. On device they would only exist in the pill (a custom View could animate; the widget cannot). Without them the frames still read.
  • Nothing comes back until midnight. Decay only moves one way inside a day, same as the counters. Grace minutes push the threshold, so the cat pauses rather than heals.
