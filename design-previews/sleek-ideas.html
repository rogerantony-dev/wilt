Sleek details that apply to any of them

  • Numbers roll. The big count animates digit-by-digit when it changes (PanelUI's text-animation has this), instead of snapping.
  • One accent at a time. The rung colour (green, amber, red) drives the ring, the glow, the number, and the pill. Nothing else on the screen is coloured.
  • Surface ladder, no borders. Depth from three tonal steps (ink, panel, panelhi) and nothing else. Hairlines only inside lists.
  • Haptics on every commit. Selection tick on rung and tab changes, a soft knock when a sheet catches. Already wired in the slider and sheet.
  • Living cat. Blink every few seconds, tail sway, eyes follow the time-left ring. It already exists in the onboarding send-off; bring it home.
  • Sheets with material. Frosted backdrop (expo-blur, one install), grabber that brightens while dragged, and content that fades in a beat after the sheet lands.
  • Ambient status bar. Tint the Android status and navigation bars to the canvas so the phone chrome disappears into the app.
  • Typography. One display face for the numbers (Inter Tight or Geist Mono, tabular) would sharpen A, B and D noticeably. That is a font asset, no native rebuild.

My pick

  • A as the base, with B's ledger inside History and D's living cat. The dial gives the screen one clear object, it reuses the ramp you already liked on the slider, and it needs only an SVG ring plus the tiles already in PanelUI.
