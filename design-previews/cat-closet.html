Dress the cat

Points become coins, earned by scrolling less and spent on clothes. The 13 unlockable cat photos go away. The wilting cat gets a body and three things to wear: a top (on its head), a body piece and a bottom. Whatever it wears shows on home, in the closet, on the block screen, and (top only) in the pill. Tap items in the closet sheet; drag the slider to watch the dressed cat wilt.

What changes

  • Goes away: the cat photo gallery, the 13 cat images (app assets and wilt_cat_* drawables), cat unlocks in milestones, and the random photo on the nudge and block screens.
  • Stays: the wilt itself. The clothes wilt with it: colours drain as the fur goes green, the hat slips as the ears droop, and once the limit is blown the outfit is grey and moth-eaten on a bone cat. My guess; easy to keep clothes pristine instead.
  • Top means headwear, because it is the one slot the pill can show. My reading of "topwear"; if you meant a shirt, Body becomes fur colour and Top becomes the shirt.
  • Coins replace points. Earned from minutes used only: 50 at 0 min, falling straight to 0 at 45 min. They land at midnight. Buying an item spends them; owned items swap for free.
  • Prices run 100 to 900; one starter per slot is free (beanie, tee, shorts). My call.
  • Native: the pill and block screen are drawn by the accessibility service, which today uses six pre-rendered head PNGs. A dressed cat means drawing the cat in Kotlin (or rendering every combination), which is the real cost of this change.
