Rules

  • Four button levels only. Primary (bone), Accent (green, for the safe choice), Glass (secondary), Ghost (dismiss). 56 tall, 16 radius, 16px semibold. Nothing else gets a border colour.
  • Glass = bone at 6% with a 10% hairline. Tiles, chips, tray, mode rows, permission cards, notes. Solid panel survives only where something has to read as a track: the island and the slider.
  • Sheets sit one tone above ink (#161615) with the same 10% hairline on the top edge, 28 radius, 62% scrim behind. Content fades in a beat after the sheet lands.
  • Onboarding gets the ambient field too, in the rung colour once one is chosen, so the first run and the home screen are one place.
